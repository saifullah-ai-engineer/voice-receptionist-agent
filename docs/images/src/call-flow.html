LANGGRAPH CALL FLOW

Five nodes, one call

The compiled graph is built once per process and reused for every call.

1

Greet

Greets the caller with the business name from business.json.

→
2

Classify

Rule-based intent classifier, with an optional LLM hook and rule fallback.

→
3

Collect

Pulls name, service, day, and time from the caller words, and checks what is missing.

→
4

Route

Decides book, answer, clarify, or hand off to a human.

→
5

Draft

Drafts the reply using only business facts and the caller words.

Classify splits into five intents

Keyword scores, phrases count double. No match means other.
bookingBook a service, an appointment, a slot.
pricingAsk what a service costs.
hoursAsk when the business opens and closes.
human_handoffTalk to a real person, a refund, a complaint.
otherAnything else, handed to a human.

Route decides the outcome

Based on intent and the details still missing.
bookSlot noted, the team calls back to confirm.
answerAnswered from the business facts.
clarifyAsks for the missing service or day.
humanConnected to a team member, with the phone number.
The classifier never invents a price. It only states the facts in business.json. 1.9 ms mean per call.
